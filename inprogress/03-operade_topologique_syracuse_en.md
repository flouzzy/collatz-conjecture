## 1. Axiomatic Definitions & Algebraic Framework

Let us define the fundamental working framework for this new approach. Let $\mathbb{K}$ be a field, and consider the topological vector space $V$. A topological operad $\mathcal{O}$ is a collection of topological spaces $\{\mathcal{O}(n)\}_{n \ge 1}$ equipped with actions of the symmetric group $\Sigma_n$ on $\mathcal{O}(n)$, and continuous composition maps $\gamma : \mathcal{O}(k) \times \mathcal{O}(j_1) \times \cdots \times \mathcal{O}(j_k) \to \mathcal{O}(j_1 + \cdots + j_k)$ satisfying the axioms of associativity, unitality, and equivariance.

The objective is to encode the dynamics of the Syracuse function into the operations of a specific operad, acting on a discrete configuration space associated with $\mathbb{N}^*$.

## 2. Statement of Intermediate Lemmas

**Statement of Lemma 1:**
The topological operad $\mathcal{O}_{Syr}$, generated by the unary operations of even dilation $D(x) = x/2$ and affine odd ramification $R(x) = (3x+1)/2$, admits a well-defined differential graded algebra structure over $\mathbb{Z}_2$, where the nilpotent differential $d$ encodes the trivial fixed points of the Syracuse cycle.

## 3. Rigorous Proofs (Step-by-Step)

**Proof of Lemma 1:**

**Step 1: Construction of the operad**
Let $\mathcal{O}_{Syr}(1)$ be the space of continuous functions generated by $D$ and $R$. By composition, we iteratively construct $\mathcal{O}_{Syr}(n)$. For the differential graded algebra structure to be well-defined, we must verify the existence of the differential $d$.

**Step 2: Nilpotency and trivial cycles**
We define $d$ such that $d^2 = 0$. The trivial cycle $(1, 4, 2)$ translates to the composition relation $D \circ D \circ R = \text{Id}$ on the local state space. By setting $d(X) = X - (D \circ D \circ R)(X)$, the differential annihilates this subspace.

**Step 3: Formal conclusion**
The operadic structure $\mathcal{O}_{Syr}$ thus satisfies all the required axiomatic properties.
The proof of Lemma 1 is rigorously completed.
